# Revision Receipt

This file is intentionally left mostly blank. It's the slot for what happens when you (Will) actually test the
live, deployed app yourself — not something to be filled in preemptively by the build process.

## What to fill in after testing

- **What broke or felt wrong in testing:** concrete, specific — a question that got a wrong/ungrounded answer, a
  citation that didn't link correctly, an upload that failed, a practice test that didn't match the practice
  problems' difficulty, UI friction, anything that didn't match the hypothesis in `HYPOTHESIS.md`.
- **What changed as a result:** the actual fix made in response, with a one-line "why," in the same spirit as
  `BUILD_LOG.md`'s entries.
